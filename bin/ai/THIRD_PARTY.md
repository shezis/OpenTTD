# Bundled AI scripts (Founder Mode)

Founder Mode uses an existing OpenTTD AI to run background city transit
operators. These packages are bundled unmodified, as downloaded from
OpenTTD's official content service (BaNaNaS). Each archive contains its
own `license.txt`.

| Archive | Author | Version | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SimpleAI-14.tar` | Brumi | 14 (2019-01-20) | GNU GPL v2 |
| `library/Pathfinder.Road-4.tar` | OpenTTD team | 4 | GNU GPL v2 |
| `library/Pathfinder.Rail-1.tar` | OpenTTD team | 1 | GNU GPL v2 |
| `library/Graph.AyStar-4.tar` | OpenTTD team | 4 | GNU GPL v2 |
| `library/Graph.AyStar-6.tar` | OpenTTD team | 6 | GNU GPL v2 |
| `library/Queue.BinaryHeap-1.tar` | OpenTTD team | 1 | GNU GPL v2 |

`FounderRival/` is not third-party: it is the rival startup AI written for this fork
(GPL v2, like the rest of OpenTTD). It plays only through the `AIFounder` API.
